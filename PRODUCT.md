# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

B2B decision-makers in Delhi NCR, India — spanning:

- **Procurement and admin managers**: vendor-selection mindset, focused on MOQ, pricing, lead time, and delivery reliability.
- **Marketing and brand managers**: care about label quality, design fidelity, and how the bottle looks on a table or at an event.
- **Founders and COOs (SMB to mid-size)**: move fast, value reliability and cost predictability, often the sole decision-maker.
- **Events and wedding coordinators**: deadline-driven, high-anxiety context, need speed, samples, and clear turnaround guarantees.

All are professional, digitally comfortable, and evaluating AquaVia against generic suppliers and Indiamart alternatives. They arrive skeptical; they leave once trust is earned.

## Product Purpose

AquaVia sells customized branded bottled water to Indian businesses — packaged drinking water, bottled by a licensed partner plant and dressed in the client's logo, colours and label design. Available in three sizes (250ml, 500ml, 1L), delivered in bulk to hotels, corporate offices, event venues, hospitals, real estate firms, gyms, and airlines across Delhi NCR, expanding pan-India.

100ml was discontinued. It is named here only so nobody reintroduces it from an old brochure — `PRODUCTS` in `src/site/data.js` is the source of truth for what is sold, and the render test asserts the discontinued sizes appear nowhere.

The website's job: convert a skeptical B2B visitor into a WhatsApp inquiry, a call, or an email to the sales desk. There is no contact form — it was removed deliberately. Trust, quality proof, and clear ordering paths are the conversion levers.

## Brand Personality

Bold, Modern, Aspirational.

The brand sits at the intersection of Indian corporate confidence and premium hospitality aesthetics. It should feel at home on a 5-star hotel meeting table and equally credible when pitched to a startup COO. Not understated luxury — aspirational conviction. The Himalayan origin is the edge: natural purity delivered with precision.

Voice: Direct. Confident. Warm without being casual. Never salesy, never generic.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS blue** (Notion, Intercom, Linear aesthetics) — over-used, corporate-anonymous, wrong register for a premium product brand.
- **Cheap B2B e-commerce / Indiamart catalog style** — cluttered, low-trust, text-heavy. The exact perception AquaVia must differentiate from.
- **Loud consumer retail** — flashy discounts, bold yellows, pop-up spam. This is a premium B2B brand, not a retail FMCG.
- **Health-food greens / organic wellness** — earthy beige and forest green suggest a different product category (herbal teas, organic grocery). Himalayan water is precision and purity, not hippie wellness.

## Design Principles

1. **Himalayan provenance as edge, not decoration.** The mountain origin is the product's core differentiator. It should be felt through imagery, language, and visual metaphor — not just mentioned once in the About section.

2. **B2B boldness over corporate boredom.** Corporate buyers are humans who respond to aspiration. Design with the confidence of a category-defining brand, not a catalog page. The dark palette and animated bottles already point this direction — lean into it.

3. **Show proof, skip promises.** Interactive tools (live customizer, bottle SVG previews), named testimonials with titles, and specific numbers (500+ brands, 2Cr+ bottles, 48hr proofing) build trust faster than superlatives. Proof goes up front; copy earns its place by being specific.

4. **Every buyer type deserves a clear path.** Procurement needs MOQ and pricing. Marketing needs label quality signals. Founders need reliability. Event coordinators need turnaround guarantees. The site should surface each of these without requiring a full linear scroll.

5. **Premium scales from SMB to enterprise.** The brand must feel credible to a luxury hotel procurement team and approachable to a 50-person startup. Confidence without exclusivity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance target. Keyboard navigability for all interactive controls (customizer, FAQ accordion, testimonials). Adequate color contrast on dark backgrounds (navy + aqua and navy + muted text). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for the floating bottle animations and scroll reveals.
